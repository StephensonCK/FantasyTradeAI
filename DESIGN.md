# Visual direction

Football operations room: athletic condensed typography, chalk-white and turf-green surfaces, chartreuse routes on an actual football play diagram, crisp data controls. The hero establishes the sport; trade inputs remain familiar and readable. Both light and dark modes preserve this identity. Barlow Condensed headings and self-hosted Geist body/data typography. No decorative motion or fabricated live metrics.

Accessibility target: WCAG 2.2 AA. Visible focus, skip link, 44px minimum controls, native select, arrow-key radio groups, combobox active descendant, text labels alongside colors, no sticky obscuring panels, reduced motion and forced-colors support. Verify both themes and 320px reflow plus loaded, empty, error, and selected-player states.
